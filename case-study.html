Case study

Role
Design and development, solo
Stack
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript
Constraints
No framework, no dependencies, no build step

Goal

Build the site for a made-up web conference that has two jobs: inform people (schedule, speakers) and get them to sign up. It had to work on a phone as well as a laptop, and I wanted to do it with nothing but the platform.

Design decisions

  • A tiny set of tokens. Five colours and one typeface, all CSS custom properties, so every page stays consistent without extra effort.
  • Frosted glass over a photo. The background blur keeps text readable on a busy image.
  • Progressive enhancement. With JavaScript off, both days of the schedule are listed one after the other and the form still validates natively.

What was tricky

  • Making the custom radio cards fully usable by keyboard while hiding the native inputs.
  • Replacing a scrolling schedule with accessible tabs: ARIA roles, arrow keys, Home and End.
  • Keeping the countdown correct before, during and after the event, without a screen reader announcing it every minute.

What I'd do next

  • A French version, with hreflang alternates.
  • WebP/AVIF images with srcset.
  • An .ics download for each talk.
